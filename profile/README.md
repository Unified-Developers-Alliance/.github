# Welcome to the Unified Developers Alliance (UDA) 🇿🇦

**Building the future of South Africa's developer ecosystem, together.**

The Unified Developers Alliance (UDA) is a central hub designed to unite coding societies, support students across South African universities, and reignite a nationwide passion for technology and programming. 

## Our Mission
We believe that learning to code shouldn't be a solitary journey. UDA exists to bridge the gap between absolute beginners and seasoned programmers by providing a unified platform for mentorship, resources, and collaboration.

## What We Do
* **The Federation of Societies:** Bringing university coding clubs and tech communities together in one central place to share resources and host inter-university events.
* **Education & Mentorship:** Providing high-quality, accessible tutorials and study materials for anyone wanting to enter the world of programming.
* **Competitions & Hackathons:** Hosting coding challenges where programmers of all skill levels can test their abilities and learn from one another.
* **Certification & Progress Tracking:** Offering structured online courses with verified certificates to acknowledge growth—from beginner to intermediate—and help members secure jobs in the tech industry.
* **Project Hosting & Showcases:** Assisting members with hosting their websites and applications, while actively monitoring and highlighting standout GitHub repositories within our community. 

## Join the Alliance
Whether you are writing your first "Hello World" or you are an experienced developer looking to mentor the next generation, there is a place for you here.
